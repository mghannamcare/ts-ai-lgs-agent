# TS AI Tender & Sales Engineer - 5 to 7 Minute Demo Script

## 1. Problem (30-45 sec)
Tender teams receive mixed files: PDF specifications, BOQs, Word scopes, drawings, images and BIM/CAD models. Review is manual, fragmented and slow, while management needs a fast Go/No-Go decision.

## 2. Product (30 sec)
TS AI Tender & Sales Engineer consolidates tender files into one workflow: extraction, requirement analysis, TS solution matching, compliance, clarifications, risk, pricing, vendor comparison and pipeline management.

## 3. Load Demo (30 sec)
Use the sidebar button **Load TS Demo Project**. Explain that all demo names/data are synthetic and only demonstrate the workflow.

## 4. Tender Analysis (2 min)
Open **Analyze Tender** after loading the demo. Show Executive Summary, Scope & BOQ, Requirements, Compliance Matrix, Risks, TS Solutions, Tender Score and source traceability.

Key message: AI output is decision support. Final engineering/commercial approval remains with TS.

## 5. Commercial Intelligence (1 min)
Open Commercial. Show ROM value, cost + contingency, expected margin, pricing coverage, procurement actions and the What-if Margin Simulator.

## 6. Executive Dashboard & Pipeline (1 min)
Open Executive Dashboard. Show pipeline value, weighted pipeline, expected GP and tender deadline alerts. Then show Sales Pipeline stages and probabilities.

## 7. Vendor Comparison (30 sec)
Explain weighted vendor ranking using price, lead time, warranty and quotation validity.

## 8. Outputs (30 sec)
Return to the analysis and generate:
- Tender Pack Excel
- Technical Proposal DOCX
- Executive Tender Assessment PDF
- JSON/CSV audit outputs

## Closing (20 sec)
The project converts tender review from disconnected manual work into a traceable AI-assisted workflow from document intake to management decision. Future production deployment would add enterprise authentication, role permissions, ERP/CRM integration and controlled company data governance.
